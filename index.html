    Four roles, one crew

    • Commander watches the radiation forecast and sends Earth reports.
    • Power engineer checks the budget and keeps the habitat warm.
    • Life support engineer manages oxygen, water and food.
    • Rover operator plans sample collection and the return to base.

    After each shift, discuss which reserve you spent and what that means for the next shift. A sol is a Martian day.
